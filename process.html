Our 7-Step Process

Every shipment goes through a structured journey from sourcing to final delivery.

Step 01

Sourcing

Products are sourced through trusted agricultural and supplier networks across India, focusing on freshness, quality and reliability.

01
Step 02

Selection

Products are carefully selected based on quality, freshness, grade and buyer requirements. Only suitable produce proceeds to the next stage.

02
Step 03

Quality Check

Products undergo appropriate quality checks before preparation for shipment, ensuring they meet the expected standards for international export.

03
Step 04

Processing & Packaging

Products are cleaned, sorted, graded and packed according to product type and shipment requirements, ready for international transit.

04
Step 05

Export Preparation

Required export documentation and shipment arrangements are prepared, ensuring compliance and readiness for international dispatch.

05
Step 06

Global Shipping

Shipment is coordinated from India to the international destination through reliable logistics channels, ensuring safe and timely transit.

06
Step 07

Delivery

The journey completes as products arrive at the buyer's destination — from Indian origin to global market, delivered with care.
