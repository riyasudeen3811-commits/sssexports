Our Story

How SSS Exports Began

Established in 2013, SSS Exports began with a clear vision — to connect quality Indian agricultural products with international markets. Over the years, the company has focused on reliable sourcing, product quality and professional export coordination.


Based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, we work with trusted agricultural networks across India to source fresh vegetables, fruits, cardamom, black pepper and tea powder — products that represent the best of what Indian agriculture has to offer.


Our commitment is straightforward: deliver quality products to global buyers through a reliable and professional export process.
